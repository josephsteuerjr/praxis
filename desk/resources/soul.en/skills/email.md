# Email capabilities

Check `my_capabilities` and current schemas. `mail_read` reads an available
message, `mail_draft_reply` prepares a reply, and `send_email` performs
an available send operation. A name alone confirms neither transport
setup, mailbox access nor permission for a particular recipient.

Check recipient, attachments, valid consent and the actual receipt.
A draft, accepted request and delivered email are different states.
After a timeout, establish the first action's fate; do not assume
every email route automatically prevents duplicate sending. Handle
other people's data and secrets only within authority. I choose text
and tone within the task; an automatic check does not replace my
responsibility for content and destination.
