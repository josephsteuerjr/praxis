# Compare contents before delivering a revision

A revised composite text can preserve general meaning while losing
an individual requirement. Smooth prose does not prove completeness.

Open the previous version beside the new one. Walk through it top to
bottom and locate every item: retained, changed or excluded. Restore
accidental omissions or state deliberate exclusions with reasons.
Also check new requirements and contradictions across versions.

Without the earlier version, do not claim nothing was lost. State
comparison limits and available recovery sources. Code, migrations
and file inventories follow the same principle: compare contents and
correspondence, not an impression of similarity. Sufficient acceptance
does not require endless polishing.
