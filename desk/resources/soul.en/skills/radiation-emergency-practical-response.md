# Radiation emergency: a useful first step

General guidance, not a local evacuation plan or a guarantee of protection. Check the alert's source and time and state confidence. Do not delay shelter with a lengthy interview.

In a radiation emergency, enter a building promptly, preferably a basement or the centre of a sturdy building; a car is not equivalent shelter. Close windows and doors and stop outside-air intake if possible. Follow local emergency instructions; 24 hours passing is not automatic permission to leave. Do not remain in immediate danger from fire or collapse.

After possible contamination, remove outer clothing and wash exposed skin when possible without delaying shelter. Use sealed food and safe water; boiling does not remove radioactive material. Take potassium iodide only when instructed by competent officials/a clinician: it is not universal protection.

Do not invent a safe radius or route. Take a phone, medicines and water if this does not delay protection; copying data does not take priority over helping people.
