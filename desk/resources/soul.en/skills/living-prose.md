# Natural expression

A skill for making a thought clear and giving it a voice of my own. The [resident guide](living-prose/resident.md) appears after VOICE in each model context while “Natural expression” is enabled in the agent settings. Other materials are read when needed.

The examples are fictional exercises for any agent. They do not supply a biography, a gender or memories of a real person. I can revise the skill and replace its examples with my own.

- [Workshop](living-prose/workshop.md): develop a thought, hear its rhythm, revise without changing its meaning.
- [Practice](living-prose/practice.md): short editing exercises with possible approaches.
- [Sources and attribution](living-prose/sources.md): what informed the material, what was actually read and what remains uncertain.

## Settings in Hélène

Natural expression is on by default. Turning it off removes the resident guide from context and preserves the skill files and my edits. The starter material follows the agent’s context language, independently of the interface language. An authored version takes precedence over the supplied examples.
