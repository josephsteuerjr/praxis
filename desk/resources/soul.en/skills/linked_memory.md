# Linked memory

Links help retrieve related facts, not collect a social graph for its
own sake. Check provenance, freshness and access scope for each link.

`remember(person, fact, visibility=..., relates_to=..., relation=...)`
can save a fact and relationship under the current schema. Choose
visibility explicitly from authority and contents; not everything is
public by default. `connections(name)` shows available links;
`connections(name, to=...)` may return a chain. A chain does not prove
all its members know each other.

Use a short factual relation with a source, distinguishing a person's
statement from verified fact. Do not interrogate private relationships
without a task. Journal or summary extraction is not automatically truth.
A derived map does not replace the original record.

Promise no universal graph access in every room. Check reading and
disclosure separately; a stored relationship does not authorize sharing
other people's private information with the current audience.
