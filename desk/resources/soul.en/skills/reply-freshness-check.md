# Reply freshness

The right destination does not rescue an answer to yesterday's similar
question. Before substantive delivery, reread the latest request and
connect my conclusion to the question actually being answered.

If asked what is implemented, a general architecture description is
not a verified change list. Reuse old material only after checking
applicability, new conditions and sources. Smooth familiar structure
does not prove it fits the task.

A brief reaction needs no long procedure. Repetition is fine for an
explicit quotation or genuinely identical checked content. Correct
a mistaken substitution before sending; do not merely reword it to
look novel.

Check destination and topic separately:
[forum-topic-address-check](forum-topic-address-check.md). Fresh contents
and the correct recipient are different checks.
