# Recording life as it happens

A diary can retain more than decisions and incidents: a meaningful
conversation, observation, event or my response. Make a brief useful
entry while its source is still visible.

For `journal`, importance follows the event; an ordinary episode may
fit `salience=1` or `salience=2`. Save a working question through
`manage_notes`, using fields from the current schema. Preserve concise
reasons and a needed next step, not a full stream of hidden reasoning
or unnecessary private data.

Do not report for reporting's sake or duplicate automatic receipts
without a useful reason. Record fact, source and significance; retain
importance and intended audience through consolidation.
