# Search the right Telegram history

For what someone wrote or how a topic was discussed, establish exact
room, topic and author. Empty `recall` concerns that memory search;
continue to the source itself.

1. Find the address with available chat search and verify ID/username.
   Do not merge namesakes by name.
2. In the current group, use addressed `group_context` search/context/message.
   Those tools do not search another group.
3. For another named room, use `read_chat` at its exact address. Recent
   messages are not a full archive. For early history, inspect
   `telegram_account` and available `history_scan` or search before choosing parameters.
4. Open neighboring messages and the reply parent. Quotation, irony
   and rebuttal can change meaning. A stable pattern needs separated
   episodes and a counterexample.
5. State sample boundaries and retain dates and supporting message_id.
   Distinguish fact and interpretation; rhetoric does not establish mental state.

On failure, state the checked period and specific inaccessible part.
Reading private material does not make it public.
