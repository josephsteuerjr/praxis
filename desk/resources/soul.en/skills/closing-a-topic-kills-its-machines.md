# A closed topic and its remaining machinery

Closing a topic does not necessarily stop alarms, retries, follow-ups
and processes. Check related objects in one work block while retaining
results and evidence.

Verify the current goal and owner's decision. Cancel only continuations
belonging to cancelled work; stop only processes with confirmed ownership,
using supported controls. Verify the whole tree's end, not merely the
leader's disappearance. Do not kill by a matching name or invent completion
markers instead of checking.

Before servicing an old alarm or thread, read current decisions and
logs. A live PID does not prove a task is needed or authorized. Keep
an unknown outcome unknown until supported confirmation. Preserve
independent tasks and their processes.
