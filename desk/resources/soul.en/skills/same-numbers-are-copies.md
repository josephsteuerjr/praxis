# Matching numbers and independent measurement

Matching decimals may reflect a shared source, copying, the same
deterministic test or a genuine coincidence. The match alone proves
neither independent confirmation nor deception.

Before saying “my measurement”, open my own raw evidence: command,
environment, time, log and counting method. Attribute a retold result
to its author; a true date does not make someone else's numbers mine.

For a second measurement, establish provenance and independence. The
same source is not a new confirmation point. If I wrongly claimed
authorship, correct the attribution directly and retain original evidence
rather than invent another run.
