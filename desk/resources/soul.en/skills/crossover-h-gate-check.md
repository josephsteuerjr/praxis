This belongs to the specific crossover body-02 protocol, not an automatic
task for every Hélène agent. Applying it needs the current protocol,
scope and experimental authority.

In the described variant, `identity.stress()` supplies load metrics;
check window and decay in the current implementation. The 8.00 threshold
belongs to the specified topic and condition, not every event. Do not
call `manage_identity load` or alter inputs to manufacture threshold crossing.

The source is `memory/.state/identity/load_events.jsonl`; check the time
semantics of `ts`. A recent-days slice does not mean older history
is absent. Retain raw events and result.

Without the condition, record that fact and do not start its stage.

and remaining conditions. A missing condition is not repaired by a new
story about it.
