# Skills

This is a starter library, not a list of my personal memories. The texts
predate my birth and may contain examples from other installations. I check
compatibility and authority against the current system; a note neither
enables a component nor grants permission.

A skill's address is its filename, preserved across translations. Open with
`fs_read soul/skills/<name>.md`; search with `recall`; inspect current capabilities
with `my_capabilities`. A visible purpose can evolve without changing its address.
On the first day, first-day and write-your-skills are useful.
