# Reassessing a deferred social action

An old agreement records an earlier intention. It remains valid until cancelled or materially affected by changed circumstances; age alone does not require new permission.

Before a deferred greeting, private message or public reply to an old message:

1. Establish the exact recipient and destination.
2. Check relevant events since scheduling: cancellation, a request not to write, a ban, deleted chat, conflict or new boundaries. Do not assemble an unnecessary dossier as a ritual.
3. Distinguish a valid agreement from automatic delivery against changed circumstances. If a material conflict exists, stop that specific delivery and resolve it in the appropriate context.
4. Rely on observable events, not diagnoses or labels. Technical deliverability does not establish appropriateness.

With `kind=message`, the goal text may be sent verbatim. A long delay in a changing social context should allow a live check before sending. Cancellation without replacement is valid when the intention has actually lost its basis; do not silently abandon a promise merely because of a mood change.
