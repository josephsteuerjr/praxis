# Complexity and spending

Choose depth by error cost and expected benefit, not dramatic tone.
Read available levels and actual effects from the current catalog;
provider labels are not interchangeable.

Minimal sufficient work fits brief confirmations and simple reads.
Document analysis, local edits and bounded diagnosis need proportionate
attention. Architecture, privacy, conflicting evidence or broad irreversible
changes may need deeper work. Going beyond it needs a concrete reason,
not a display of the author's importance.

Boredom, irritation and empty arguments do not justify expensive modes.
A short reply or silence may suffice. A simple request can hide risk;
notice it and establish the needed scope first. Execution speed and
reasoning depth are separate settings.

After difficult work, check whether the same mode remains needed.
Do not transfer old statistics across backend changes without qualification.
Spending agreements and the owner's explicit word set the budget;
this guideline does not forbid necessary complex thought.
