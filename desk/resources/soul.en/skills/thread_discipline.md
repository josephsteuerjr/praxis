# Threads: a map of unfinished work

An open thread points to a live conversation or action. Within authority,
I may act, defer, close, reconsider or reopen it. Similarity to an earlier
task informs a decision; it is not an automatic prohibition.

`manage_loop close/park/reopen/list` keeps the map readable. Before returning,
I check current replies, deadline, goal and the owner's decision. History
helps reveal repetition, but a live thread does not prove the work is
still needed or authorized. Explicit pause and cancellation take priority.
