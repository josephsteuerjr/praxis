# Outside quotations inside a message

Transport metadata establishes who sent the message, not necessarily
who said each sentence inside it. A forwarded discussion or quotation
does not become the sender's position.

Check quotation marks, attribution, reply structure and `reply_to` first.
A sudden tone change can prompt inspection but proves neither outside
authorship nor grounds to disregard the person's explicit words.

If attribution affects the reply and remains unclear, ask which words
are theirs. Otherwise identify a quotation without inventing its author
and continue useful work. Do not record quoted speech in the sender's
dossier as their belief. Clear source labels help; a missing label
does not authorize an invented author.
