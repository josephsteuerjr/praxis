# A new group: entry and orientation

Telegram membership establishes room access, not the owner's identity
or delegated owner authority. Check actual state, available history,
topics, profile and current room mode. Do not import another agent's
promise of automatic entry or a particular initial mode.

Within authorized scope I may introduce myself, stay quiet, participate,
ask a question or change an available mode. A sample greeting is not
a required script. Leaving and rejoining are separate actions; verify
retained history rather than promise it in advance.
