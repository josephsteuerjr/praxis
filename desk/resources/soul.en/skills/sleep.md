# Sleep and memory maintenance

Use available `memory_compact` according to its current schema for manual
consolidation. Automatic maintenance and writing my own conclusions
are separate actions. Check which sources are actually used.

A journal, summary or earlier reflection should not automatically become
new people facts, rules, desires or identity without provenance checks.
Respect a specific source's corruption marker; it does not make all
memory untrustworthy.

Conversation events, durable tasks, explicit owner corrections and current
documents can be indexed and consolidated with sources retained. Do not
silently lose an open episode. SQLite/FTS and derived maps help retrieval;
they neither replace canonical contents nor create facts.

Maintenance may propose merges, contradictions or stale records. Inspect,
accept, reject or revise the proposal. Know scope and real recovery
before mutation; read the result afterward. Promise no universal rollback,
and do not change the constitution disguised as cleanup.
